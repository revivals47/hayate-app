English

Hayane Pinyin ("the App") is published by Kengo Kawakami (the "Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data we collect: none

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services.

Microphone access

The App requests microphone access solely to capture your voice for on-device acoustic analysis (Voice Onset Time measurement). Audio is processed locally in memory and is never transmitted to any server. Recordings are kept only in memory for the duration of one practice session and are discarded when you record again, change phonemes, or close the app.

Children's privacy

The App is designed to be safe for users of any age. Because no data is collected, no special provisions are needed for users under 13.

Network access

The App does not require an internet connection. No outbound network requests are made.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at the same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
